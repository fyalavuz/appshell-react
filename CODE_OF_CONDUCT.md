# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Critique code, not
people.

## What we expect

- Welcoming, patient language — especially with newcomers and non-native
  English speakers.
- Feedback that is specific and about the work: "this breaks on iOS Safari
  because…" rather than "this is wrong".
- Accepting that maintainers may say no. A closed issue is not a verdict on
  the person who opened it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any other
space where someone represents this project.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainer at
<https://github.com/fyalavuz>. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers
who do not act in good faith on a report may themselves face consequences.

Responses are proportionate: usually a private word, sometimes a warning,
and for repeated or severe behaviour, a block from the project's spaces.
